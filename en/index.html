About

The KRAKEN method

I’m Ezequiel, and people know me as KRAKEN. I hold a degree in Business Administration and I’m studying Artificial Intelligence, but my obsession has always been training: I dove in, studying and reading everything I could find about physical development until I became a personal trainer.

Read more

The nickname sums up how I see things. To reach your best version you have to move like a cephalopod: juggle eight things at once — studying, working, traveling, your relationships, your goals — and still make time to train. The question is how to do all that without breaking down.

After years of research I built a method focused on what matters: maximum physical development with the minimum time investment, taking care of your body. Damage doesn’t come from doing something extraordinary; it comes from repeating the same thing over and over — like water shaping rock through persistence, not force. Training intelligently is what keeps you healthy in the long run.

My mission is to help those who fight for their dreams get a little closer to their goals. With the KRAKEN method, it can be done.
