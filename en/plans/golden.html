Frequently asked questions

Do I need an app account?

Yes. For Golden to be linked to your user, you have to sign in to the app with your account when you pay. If you don’t have one yet, create it first with the same email.

Is it a one-time or a recurring payment?

It’s a subscription: it renews automatically each period (monthly or yearly) until you cancel.

How do I cancel?

Whenever you want, using the “Botón de baja de servicio” (Spanish page), with no penalty and no notice period. It stops renewals and you keep access until the end of the period you already paid.

Which payment methods can I use?

Mercado Pago, which charges in Argentine pesos, or PayPal, which charges in US dollars.

What’s the difference between monthly and yearly?

The same features; yearly is better value (you save around 35% compared to paying month by month).
